AC-39, the falsifier slot of a scenario without counter evidence, branch block7/field-record

WHAT WAS MISSING. `No counter evidence recorded` existed in no catalog key and no component, and no
completeness counter existed anywhere. R-40 asks for both: the slot prints that text, the surface's
gap register carries the scenario id and the field id, and no threshold and no other scenario's
falsifier prints.

WHAT WAS BUILT.
- The same slot as AC-38: a scenario with no falsifier, or with one that misses a key or whose metric
  the payload does not hold, prints `No counter evidence recorded` in the falsifier slot. The scenario
  stays on the card and the other five facts print unchanged.
- src/lib/falsifier.ts `falsifierGaps(scenarios, fieldId, date)` returns one gap row per scenario
  without a falsifier, carrying both ids:
    section counter_evidence, cause `scenario <id> of field <field id> carries no falsifier`,
    source CO-B6-02, status missing, remediation CO-B6-02.
  The surface merges those rows into the gap register it already prints, after the payload's own gaps.
- src/components/NumbersView.tsx `CounterEvidenceCard`, rendered as the eighth card of the grid
  (columns 1 to 12 at 1440, 1 to 6 at 780, full width at 360, per Table E of the spec), prints one
  line per scenario, the falsifier text or the abstention, and the completeness line. For a polygon
  with no fixture scenario it prints the empty text naming that a polygon has no rotation scenario.
- src/lib/falsifier.ts `falsifierCompleteness` reports `{ total, withFalsifier, missing, status }`,
  where status is `passed` only when every scenario on screen carries a falsifier and `not verified`
  otherwise.
