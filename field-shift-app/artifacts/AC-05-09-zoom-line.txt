AC-05 and AC-09, the zoom line over the tiers that are mounted, round 2 rework

WHAT WAS WRONG. `polygonStatusLines` took the line's number from `lowestMountedZoom(scene.tiers)`, and
the scene's `tiers` carries the Table B overview numbers in every Brazil mode. Mode B mounts no
overview file (the source carries no overview level, M-02), so the line printed `Polygons from zoom 5`
while the lowest mounted tier was the detail at 8. The gate measured it at zoom 6, 7, 9 and 10.

FIX. `mountedTiers(scene)` (src/lib/polygon-source.ts) returns the tier contract with `overview: null`
unless the scene also carries an `overviewUrl`, and returns null when no tier's URL is mounted. The
status lines take the number from that value, so the line names what is on the map and the no-tier
state prints the R-11 failure line with neither R-12 line.
