## Problem

Block 5 makes a real polygon the selected field of the app and prints five rows about it: class, area, basis, class year, source. It cannot print a single climate, soil, vegetation or rotation value, because block 5 R-49 reserves all of them for this block. The screen therefore answers "what is this polygon" and not "what happened on it".

Block 6 answers the second question with numbers that survive a hostile reader. Each number names its source, its date, its basis and its native resolution; each section abstains instead of defaulting; a missing input produces a named gap, not a zero. The trap this spec is written against is a screen full of precise looking numbers that are really one 9 km cell repeated for 88 polygons, one 250 m soil model sampled at a point, and one reanalysis grid cell standing in for a farm. Every coarse value is therefore printed with its resolution and with the shared cell warning, and no coarse value is drawn as pixels.

The second trap is the extraction route. Reading the colour of a served PNG tile gives a proportion of a palette, not a physical value. Block 6 uses that route for exactly one series, labels it as a palette, and puts every physical value on the second route: a zonal statistic over a cloud optimised GeoTIFF, or a documented API, or an offline sidecar.

## Users and scope

- Users: the producer who reads the numbers to decide the next planting window, and the judge who checks that a number cannot be read without its source, its basis, its date and its resolution.

### Provenance, basis and honesty

- R-07 (ubiquitous): Every number the surface prints SHALL have exactly one row in the payload's `provenance` array, and that row SHALL carry `metric` (an id of Data contracts Table A), `label`, `value`, `unit`, `basis`, `as_of`, `source`, `resolution`, `route` and `fetched_at`. A printed number without its row fails the block, and a row for a number that is not printed fails it too. `metric` is the only key: no row SHALL be keyed by its label, its position or its unit. A chart series SHALL be one row for its family, covering every point it draws, because a point is a graphical mark of that one measurement and its own date is printed in the chart; a value printed as text, a card row or a table cell SHALL carry its own row.
- R-08 (ubiquitous): The basis label of every row SHALL be the label of that row's own `basis`, one of `observed`, `retrieved`, `modelled` and `entered`, and presentation SHALL follow block 1 R-14 unchanged in its two classes: `observed`, `retrieved` and `entered` present as measured; `modelled` presents beside the `modelled` label and SHALL NOT be presented as measured. The row's own `basis` is the only input to the label.
- R-09 (ubiquitous): No control, global or per field, SHALL set, clear or override the basis label, the value, the `as_of`, the `resolution` or the `route` of any row of this block, and no label SHALL be derived by matching a pattern in a metric id, a label, a source string or a layer name. This carries block 1 R-15 to every row of R-07 and forbids the one shortcut that would pass a naive test: a metric whose id contains `smap` SHALL NOT be marked `retrieved` by that substring, and a metric whose source contains `POWER` SHALL NOT be marked `modelled` by that substring; the label comes from the row.
- R-10 (ubiquitous): Every row whose native resolution is coarser than the polygon SHALL print that resolution in the row and in its card header, and the three coarse gridded sources SHALL print that their value is not a measurement of the polygon: POWER is a reanalysis cell of about half a degree and the three fixture fields fall in one cell (M-01, M-11); a SMAP 9 km cell is about 8,100 ha and about 88 of the 900 polygons measured at the field point share one value (M-09, M-10); SoilGrids is a static 250 m model sampled at the centroid. No coarse value SHALL be presented as a measurement of the polygon, and no card SHALL hide the resolution of the value it shows.
- R-11 (ubiquitous): Every new visible string and every new accessible name SHALL come from `src/strings/en.ts` through `t()` in the same change, `src/strings/pt-BR.ts` SHALL stay empty as block 3 R-37 leaves it, and the class names, unit symbols and source strings that are data values SHALL come from Data contracts Tables A and D rather than from the catalog. The one exception is recorded beside both tables.
- R-12 (ubiquitous): A number whose input is missing SHALL abstain. The row's `value` SHALL be `null`, the surface SHALL print the abstention text naming the missing input and its source, and the gap register SHALL carry the reason (R-41). No zero, no default, no previous value, no neighbouring polygon's value and no fixture value SHALL stand in for a missing input.
- R-13 (ubiquitous): Each data card of the surface (climate, water balance, SMAP, NDVI, soil, rotation, municipality) SHALL print its own source line above its numbers, carrying the source URL or the sidecar file, the `as_of` date of the data and the basis of the section, so that a reader who sees one card without the provenance table still sees the three facts. The counter evidence card, the provenance table and the gap register take their facts from the sections and from the payload and carry no source line of their own. A data card whose source line is missing fails this requirement even when its rows are complete.

### Climate series and aggregates (F-08, F-09, F-35)

- R-16 (ubiquitous): The window SHALL be 42 consecutive days ending on the last day at or before the chosen date on which `PRECTOTCORR` carries a value other than the fill value `-999`, because rain is the anchor of the climate card and of the water balance's `in`. Every other parameter SHALL be checked per aggregate by R-19 and per water balance term by R-23, so the window does not move for a parameter that one aggregate needs alone. The surface SHALL print the window start, the window end and the count of days on which `PRECTOTCORR` is valid, and when the chosen date is later than the last consolidated day it SHALL print the number of requested days that were dropped as unconsolidated. The window SHALL NOT move to a neighbouring year and SHALL NOT be shortened silently.
- R-17 (ubiquitous): The climate section SHALL read the POWER daily point API (`https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/daily/point`), community `AG`, at the polygon's centroid, for the sixteen parameters and units of Data contracts Table A, and SHALL request no parameter outside that table. The fill value `-999` SHALL be filtered before any arithmetic, any chart point and any printed value, so that no aggregate, mean or extreme contains it.
- R-18 (ubiquitous): The aggregates SHALL be the six named functions of Table A, computed over the window and rounded to that table's decimals: rain total (the sum of `PRECTOTCORR`, mm), longest dry spell (the longest run of consecutive days with `PRECTOTCORR` below 1.0 mm, days), growing degree days base 10 (the sum of `max(0, (T2M_MAX + T2M_MIN) / 2 - 10)`, C day), frost days (the sum of `FROST_DAYS`, days), minimum temperature (the minimum of `T2M_MIN`, C) and mean root wetness (the mean of `GWETROOT`, unit 1). The worked example of Data contracts Table B reproduces the oracle of Table H exactly.
- R-19 (state-driven): While fewer than 80 percent of the window days carry a non fill value for the parameters an aggregate needs, that aggregate SHALL abstain with a gap naming the parameter, the valid day count and the missing day count; at or above 80 percent it SHALL print, with the valid day count beside it. The threshold is reused from block 1 R-07 as a coverage rule, and it applies per aggregate, not per section.
- R-20 (ubiquitous): The normals SHALL read the same calendar window (the same start month and day, the same end month and day) in each of the thirty five years 1991 to 2025 at the same point, and SHALL print the median, the minimum and the maximum of the rain total, the rank of the current total among those 35 earlier years, the percentile defined as `round(100 * rank / 35)` with `rank` equal to the number of earlier years whose total is strictly lower, the count of days with `T2M_MAX` at or above 35 C in the current window, and the median of that count over the 35 years. The card SHALL print the statement that the source is reanalysis, that is `modelled`, on a cell of about half a degree.
- R-21 (ubiquitous): The climate card SHALL print a daily chart with one point per valid day for rain, `T2M_MAX` and `T2M_MIN`, each axis in the unit of Table A, and SHALL NOT draw a line across a day without a value. A dropped day SHALL be a gap in the series and SHALL appear in the counts of R-16.

### Water balance (F-10)

- R-22 (ubiquitous): The water balance SHALL print exactly three numbers in millimetres over the window: `in`, the sum of `PRECTOTCORR`; `out`, the sum of `EVLAND` over the same days; and `remaining`, `in` minus `out`. The card SHALL print the formula, the window, the unit and the statement that the balance is an estimate built from modelled fluxes and not a measurement.
- R-23 (unwanted behaviour): If any day of the window lacks a non fill `PRECTOTCORR` or `EVLAND` value, the balance SHALL abstain as a whole: all three numbers SHALL be `null`, the card SHALL print the abstention text naming the missing parameter and the number of missing days, and no partial sum, default, zero or SMAP value SHALL stand in for a missing input. The SMAP series SHALL NOT enter either side of the arithmetic; it is printed as a separate anchor and labelled as one.
- R-24 (ubiquitous): `out` SHALL be labelled as POWER `EVLAND`, land surface evaporation, and the card SHALL state that it is not a crop water demand and that no reference evapotranspiration is used, because no measured reference ET exists in this repository's sources (OD-02). The statement SHALL sit beside the number, not in a footnote that the card can hide.

### SMAP series (F-08 series half)

- R-25 (state-driven): The SMAP series SHALL be read from the block 4 GIBS raster layers through the block 4 tile proxy, one value per day for the surface layer (`SMAP_L3_Passive_Enhanced_Day_Soil_Moisture`) and the root zone layer (`SMAP_L4_Analyzed_Root_Zone_Soil_Moisture`), and it SHALL be presented as the palette position of the served tile on a 0 to 100 percent axis, with the source resolution (9 km posting over a 36 km radiometer footprint for the surface layer, 9 km for the L4 layers), the shared cell warning of R-10, and the statement `palette, not a physical unit`. The series SHALL NOT print a volumetric water content, an `m3/m3` unit, a soil wetness index or any physical value, because the proxy serves a rendered image and inverting its palette would invent a precision the source does not carry.
- R-26 (unwanted behaviour): If the tile proxy answers a status outside 200, or the layer declares a date for which no tile is served, that day SHALL be a gap in the SMAP series and the section SHALL record a gap row naming the status and the address. The section SHALL NOT interpolate, SHALL NOT repeat the previous day's value, SHALL NOT tint a missing day and SHALL NOT fall back to a fixture value.

### NDVI series (F-36)

- R-27 (ubiquitous): The NDVI series SHALL be built from the Element84 Earth Search STAC API (`https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/search`), collection `sentinel-2-l2a`, with the polygon's ring as the coordinates of a GeoJSON `Polygon` in `intersects` and the window start and end as `datetime`, and from the `B04`, `B08` and `SCL` assets of each returned scene, read by HTTP range request against the cloud optimised GeoTIFF. No login, no key and no Google asset SHALL be used.
- R-28 (ubiquitous): A scene SHALL enter the series when its `eo:cloud_cover` is below 20 percent. For each such scene the section SHALL compute NDVI per pixel as `(B08 - B04) / (B08 + B04)` on the asset's own reflectance, keeping only pixels whose `SCL` class is 4, 5 or 7 and masking every other class, and SHALL report the mean over the kept pixels, the kept pixel count, the polygon pixel count and `valid_fraction`, the kept count divided by the polygon count. A point whose `valid_fraction` is below 0.5 SHALL print marked as partial and SHALL NOT enter a section summary.
- R-29 (state-driven): While fewer than two scenes with `valid_fraction` at or above 0.5 exist in the window, the NDVI section SHALL abstain with a gap naming the scene count and the best valid fraction. It SHALL NOT draw a one point trend, a monthly mean, a MODIS substitute or a value carried from another polygon.
- R-30 (ubiquitous): The NDVI card SHALL print the scene count found, the scene count below 20 percent cloud, the point count plotted, the `valid_fraction` of each point, and a source line naming Sentinel-2, the `sentinel-2-l2a` collection and the three assets. The MODIS NDVI layer of the block 4 registry SHALL NOT be used as a per polygon value anywhere in this block.

### Soil card (F-37)

- R-31 (ubiquitous): The soil card SHALL read SoilGrids (`https://rest.isric.org/soilgrids/v2.0/properties/query`) at the polygon's centroid for `clay`, `sand`, `phh2o` and `soc` at the depths `0-5cm`, `5-15cm` and `15-30cm`, SHALL divide every returned mean by the `d_factor` returned in the same response, and SHALL print clay and sand in g/kg, pH as pH and soil organic carbon in g/kg at the three depths, with the decimals of Data contracts Table A, together with the statement that the source is a static 250 m model and not a map.
- R-32 (unwanted behaviour): If the SoilGrids response lacks a property, a depth or a `d_factor`, the card SHALL print the abstention text for the affected rows and record a gap naming what was missing. It SHALL NOT reuse a value across depths or properties, SHALL NOT convert with a hardcoded factor of 10 when the response carries none, and SHALL NOT copy the 0 to 5 cm row into the deeper rows.

### Rotation history (F-38)

- R-33 (ubiquitous): The rotation history SHALL be read from the polygon sidecar table `polygon_mapbiomas`, the build product of CO-B6-01, and never from the MapBiomas storage bucket at runtime, because the anonymous bucket answers 401 and 403 (M-07). The table SHALL be keyed by `polygon_id` (the region prefixed id of block 5) and SHALL carry, per row, `region`, `product`, `code`, `class_name`, `year`, `collection`, `area_ha`, `fraction`, `basis` and `source`.
- R-34 (ubiquitous): Every class SHALL be identified by the pair `product` and `code` and never by the code alone, because code 1 exists only as second crop corn, and codes 41 and 62 carry different meanings in the first crop product and the second crop product (Data contracts Table D). A lookup that resolves a code without its product fails this requirement, and the sidecar's primary key SHALL be `(polygon_id, product, year)`.
- R-35 (state-driven): The first crop history SHALL print one row per year the sidecar carries between 1985 and 2025, with the class name of Table D and the year; the second crop history SHALL print one row per year between 2000 and 2025 for the ten states the product maps, and SHALL abstain with a gap naming the state when the polygon is outside them. The card SHALL print `MapBiomas Collection 11` and the collection year as its provenance line, and SHALL NOT print the Trazo3 `mbMode 2024, collection not verified` line, which belongs to a different source, a different year and an unverified collection (M-08).
- R-36 (state-driven): While the polygon has no sidecar row, the rotation section SHALL abstain with the gap `no MapBiomas Collection 11 row for this polygon`. It SHALL NOT fall back to `mbmode24`, to a neighbouring polygon's class, to the fixture crop history or to a national percentage, and it SHALL NOT print a year it does not hold.

### Municipal context (F-39)

- R-37 (ubiquitous): The municipal context SHALL read the IBGE SIDRA table 1612 through its API v3 (`https://servicodados.ibge.gov.br/api/v3/agregados/1612`), variable 109 (planted area, hectares), product category 2713 (soybean, `Soja (em grão)`), for the polygon's municipality at level `N6` and for its state at level `N3`, for the reference years of Data contracts Table H. It SHALL print the municipality row with its year, its code and its source URL, the state row labelled as a state total that is not this field, and the polygon's area as a share of the municipal planted area only when both numbers exist, printed as a percentage of the municipal area and never as a statement about the field's yield or the state's production. Until CO-B6-06 lands, the municipality row and the share abstain with a gap naming the missing join, and only the state row prints; that abstention is the data dependency CO-B6-06 names, not a render defect.
- R-38 (unwanted behaviour): If the municipality code is unresolved, or the SIDRA response carries no value for the year, the municipal row and the share SHALL abstain with a gap naming the reason, and the state row SHALL still print labelled as the state total. The surface SHALL NOT infer the municipality from a name, a nearest centroid, the region adapter or a shapefile it does not build, and SHALL NOT print a municipal value for the state.

### Counter evidence and the gap register (F-13, F-14)

- R-39 (ubiquitous): Every rotation scenario SHALL carry a `falsifier` object in the scenarios response, with `metric` (a metric id of Table A), `operator` (one of `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`), `threshold`, `unit` and `text_key`, all five required. The rotation card's back face SHALL print it as the sixth labelled field, `Falsifier`, immediately after `Benefit`, together with the current value of that metric from the numbers payload of the same field and that value's own basis label and date. The card SHALL NOT print a falsifier whose metric has no row in that payload.
- R-40 (unwanted behaviour): If a scenario carries no `falsifier`, the card SHALL print the label `No counter evidence recorded` in the falsifier slot and the surface's gap register SHALL carry the scenario id and the field id. The card SHALL NOT hide the scenario, SHALL NOT invent a threshold, SHALL NOT reuse another scenario's falsifier and SHALL NOT compute a falsifier from a metric the payload does not hold. The fixture completeness of this requirement is the build product of CO-B6-02, and the criterion that reads it reports not verified until that card lands.
- R-41 (ubiquitous): The surface SHALL carry a gap register with one row per gap, each row naming the section, the cause, the source or file that failed, the status or error text, the date and the remediation (the carry-over id when one exists). A gap row SHALL appear only when its condition is measured, which is an absent row, a failed request, a missing parameter, an out of range state or a scene count below the minimum, and SHALL NOT appear when the data is present. With no gap the register SHALL print its empty text and no row.
- R-42 (ubiquitous): The payload's `gaps` array and the printed register SHALL be one list: every gap the backend records SHALL print, and the app SHALL NOT add, drop, merge, reorder or reword a gap. The register's order SHALL be the payload's order.

### Extraction per polygon (F-11)

- R-43 (ubiquitous): A physical per polygon value SHALL come only from route 2 of Data contracts Table F: a zonal statistic on a cloud optimised GeoTIFF at the polygon's own ring (the NDVI of R-28), a documented point or mean API (POWER, SoilGrids, SIDRA), or the offline sidecar (MapBiomas). Route 1, the canvas of the served tiles, SHALL be used only for the SMAP palette series of R-25 and SHALL never produce a physical value, an area, a class or a number with a unit.
- R-44 (ubiquitous): The surface SHALL print the disclosure that route 1 carries the palette of the served image and not the physical unit, in the SMAP card and beside every route 1 value. The two routes SHALL NOT be compared numerically in the app or in a criterion, because a palette proportion and a physical value are different quantities; F-11's 10 percent cross route acceptance is withdrawn by this requirement and carried as CO-B6-08.

### The numbers route, its cache and its offline behaviour

- R-45 (ubiquitous): The backend SHALL answer exactly one new route, `POST /fields/{field_id}/numbers`, with the request of Data contracts Table B: `field_id` a fixture field id, a `trazo:` id or a `csb:` id; the body `{ring, centroid, area_ha, date}`; and the response the payload of Table B and Table E, whose data sections carry `state` in `ok`, `abstained`, `not_applicable` or `unavailable`, while `query`, `provenance` and `gaps` are always present and carry no state. The route SHALL accept every date from 2015-04-01 to the current day, which is block 4's axis start and today.
- R-46 (ubiquitous): The route SHALL answer 200 with the payload when at least one section is `ok`, and SHALL NOT fail the whole request because one source failed: the failed section SHALL carry `state` `unavailable`, its `error` (the HTTP status, the socket error class or the missing file path) and one gap row, while every other section keeps its values. The route SHALL answer 400 for a date outside the accepted range, a ring with fewer than three points, a ring whose signed area is zero, or a centroid outside the ring; 404 for a field id that resolves to no fixture field and no polygon of the active region; and 422 for a body that does not match the schema. Those are the only non 200 answers.
- R-47 (ubiquitous): The route SHALL cache per source with the lifetimes of Table B and SHALL expose them: `cached` and `fetched_at` per section, a second request inside a section's lifetime returning the same `fetched_at` and the same values, a request after it refreshing that section alone, and no cache write for a section whose `state` is `unavailable`. `fetched_at` SHALL be the instant of the upstream read, not the instant of the response.
- R-48 (state-driven): While the network is unavailable, the route SHALL answer 200 with every section `unavailable` and one gap per section naming the offline state, and the surface SHALL print the values it already holds for the same field and the same window marked `last read {date}`, or its empty state when it holds none. It SHALL NOT print a stale value as current, SHALL NOT read a value from the fixture for a polygon, and SHALL NOT extend a `fetched_at`.

### A polygon with no fixture scenario

- R-49 (state-driven): While the selected field is a polygon with no fixture scenario, the numbers surface SHALL print the sections of R-16 to R-38 that its sources support, the provenance table and the gap register, SHALL print the counter evidence empty text naming that a polygon has no rotation scenario, and SHALL NOT print a scenario name, a falsifier, a fixture evidence row or a fixture coverage number. On the map screen the rotation cards and the evidence panel SHALL keep block 5 R-26 exactly as frozen: the existing empty text, no `/fields/{id}/scenarios` request for the polygon, no fixture value, and no coverage pill. Block 6 SHALL NOT change `ScenarioCards.tsx`, `EvidencePanel.tsx`, `CommandToggle.tsx`, `SupportingDataToggle.tsx`, `AgentBar.tsx` or the coverage pill, and its only change to `RotationFlipCard.tsx` SHALL be the sixth field of R-39.
- R-50 (unwanted behaviour): If the area of the ring the request carries differs from the area the polygon's own source reports by more than 5 percent, every zonal value computed from that ring SHALL be marked `fragment`, the card SHALL print both areas and the percentage, and a gap row SHALL record the clip. The mark SHALL NOT be applied when the two areas agree within 5 percent, and a fragment value SHALL NOT print unmarked as the field's value.

### The default region and the region of a field id

- R-51 (ubiquitous): The default region of this block SHALL be the United States, the value block 5 R-01 defaults `regionId` to, and a field id SHALL resolve by its own naming and never by a fallback to Brazil: the prefix `csb:` selects the United States and the prefix `trazo:` selects Brazil; a fixture id resolves in the `region` its fixture entry carries, which every fixture entry carries, because a fixture field is a demonstration field and its region is part of the demonstration; and an id that carries no prefix and no fixture entry resolves in the United States.
